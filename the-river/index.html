Southwestern Ethiopia

The Omo rises in the Ethiopian Highlands and runs south to Lake Turkana. No river leaves the lake. The Omo ends there.

Lower Omo

The last stretch before the lake. Woodland follows the banks; open savannah and thicket stand behind it. Each year the river rises over its banks, and farmers plant the wet ground it leaves.

An old valley

Fossils of early humans lie in its sediments, and UNESCO listed the Lower Valley of the Omo in 1980. At least 200,000 people live here today. The Mursi, Hamar, Bodi, Kara, Kwegu, Nyangatom and Dasanech are among them.

The Valley

From its source in the Highlands to Lake Turkana, the Omo falls about 1,900 metres. The valley is where that fall ends.
